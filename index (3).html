Orders & delivery

Keep rental orders connected from setup to follow-up

Practical handoff checkpoints for equipment, delivery records and billing context.

Sample blog · Prepared for the SunDME website.

Rental workflows continue after the first delivery. Teams need to understand which equipment belongs to the order, what has been delivered and what context the billing team needs next. A connected record helps keep those details together.

Start with a clear order context

Make the item, patient, payer and order status easy to identify. Keep the supporting documentation and review status linked to that record so staff can trace an update to its source.

Connect fulfillment to the order

When equipment is assigned or dispatched, record the relevant item and delivery details against the order. Link delivery evidence where applicable. A delivery record should help the next team member understand what happened, without relying on a separate message thread.

Plan for changes

  • Record equipment changes with the relevant date and reason.
  • Capture returns or discontinuations and identify the next action.
  • Keep unresolved documentation questions visible to the responsible team.
  • Review the billing context when order or delivery information changes.

Make the next handoff explicit

Before preparing claim data, confirm that the appropriate operational reviews are complete. A shared status, owner and follow-up date can make exceptions easier to work through.

Map the workflow before rollout

Walk through a representative rental order with the SunDME team. Review how patient records, supporting documents, item assignments and delivery information relate to your billing process, and confirm the scope for your environment.

This sample article focuses on operational coordination. It does not set billing frequency, coverage or payer-specific rental rules.
