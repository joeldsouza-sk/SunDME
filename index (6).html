What is SunDME?

SunDME is revenue cycle management software for durable medical equipment (DME) and home medical equipment (HME) providers. It connects patient intake, documents, orders, delivery records, claim preparation, payments and denial follow-up in one back-office workflow.

Who is SunDME designed for?

SunDME is designed primarily for small to mid-sized DME/HME providers and their billing, revenue cycle and operations teams. It supports teams coordinating documentation, equipment orders, delivery and billing handoffs.

Which revenue cycle functions does SunDME cover?

SunDME covers seven connected areas: patient intake and onboarding; document management; orders and prescriptions; basic inventory and delivery tracking; claim preparation; payment posting and reconciliation; and denial capture and follow-up. Confirm the modules and configuration for your organization during the demo.

How is SunDME different from general medical billing software?

SunDME focuses on DME-specific documentation and the links between a patient, prescription, equipment order, delivery, claim and payment. Its workflow is designed around DME operations and billing handoffs, including respiratory equipment, resupply and rental scenarios where applicable.

What documents can SunDME organize?

SunDME organizes prescriptions and supporting DME records, such as standard written orders (SWOs), clinical notes, prior authorization records, advance beneficiary notices (ABNs) and proof of delivery (POD). Documents are linked to the patient and order. The documents needed depend on the item, payer and circumstances.

Does SunDME support prior authorization tracking?

SunDME supports tracking authorization records and linking them to the relevant order and claim context. This helps teams review documentation readiness. It does not guarantee authorization approval; confirm the authorization workflow and any payer connections for your setup.

Can SunDME support CPAP/BiPAP, resupply and rental workflows?

SunDME is designed around DME use cases that include CPAP/BiPAP onboarding, resupply and rental tracking. The applicable workflow, documentation and billing steps depend on your equipment categories, payer requirements and configured scope.

How does SunDME connect delivery records to billing?

SunDME links item assignments, dispatch information, delivery details and applicable proof-of-delivery attachments to the order. This gives the billing team a traceable path from the delivered equipment to the supporting record when preparing claim data.

Does SunDME submit claims directly to every payer?

SunDME prepares structured claim and invoice data from connected orders, documents and delivery records. Claim submission depends on the agreed external clearinghouse and payer setup. Confirm supported connections and submission scope before implementation.

Can SunDME integrate with an EHR, EMR or clearinghouse?

Integration availability depends on your existing systems and the agreed project scope. Share your EHR, EMR, clearinghouse and payer requirements with the SunDME team so the supported connections, data flows and responsibilities can be confirmed before onboarding.

How does SunDME handle payments and outstanding balances?

SunDME records payments, adjustments and write-offs at the claim or service-line level. Teams can review paid, partially paid and outstanding balances with the related claim context. Confirm remittance handling and reconciliation workflows for your configuration.

How does SunDME support denial management?

SunDME captures denial reasons and status codes, links them to the relevant claim or service line and supporting records, and supports follow-up and resolution tracking. It helps teams review the upstream context; it does not guarantee that a payer will reverse a denial or make payment.

How should we evaluate SunDME security and PHI safeguards?

Before onboarding protected health information (PHI), review the controls and agreements for your deployment with the SunDME team. Ask about role-based access, audit logging, encryption, data segregation, backups and any applicable business associate agreement. Confirm the implemented controls and responsibilities rather than relying on a general compliance label.

What does SunDME onboarding and training involve?

SunDME uses phased onboarding: align the workflow and data plan, train key users and adopt selected workflows, then expand coverage. Migration depth, configuration and integration requirements shape the timeline. The initial discussion defines the implementation scope and support arrangements.

Does SunDME replace our billing or operations team?

No. SunDME supports your existing teams with connected records, checklists, readiness indicators and structured tracking. People remain responsible for reviews, exceptions and payer-specific decisions; the platform is not presented as zero-touch billing.
