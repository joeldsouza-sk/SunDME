One connected revenue cycle

From first intake
to the next follow-up.

SunDME keeps every step connected, so your team can follow the whole story without piecing it together.

01 / Patient intake & onboarding

Start with a connected patient record.

Capture patient demographics, insurance details and referral context in a structured intake workflow.

  • Patient and payer information
  • Referral and prescription context
  • Intake checklists and follow-up ownership

02 / DME document management

The right documents. In the right context.

Organize prescriptions, clinical notes and other DME documents around the patient and order.

  • Patient and order document linkage
  • Prescription and clinical-note context
  • Authorization and delivery documents

03 / Orders & prescriptions

Keep the order moving with context.

Track orders from prescription through fulfillment, with item information and documentation readiness connected.

  • Order lifecycle tracking
  • HCPCS and item-group context
  • Documentation readiness review

04 / Inventory & delivery

Know what went where, and for whom.

Connect item assignments, dispatch details and proof of delivery to the order they support.

  • Basic inventory and item assignment
  • Dispatch and delivery records
  • Proof-of-delivery attachments

05 / Claim preparation

Build claims on a connected foundation.

Prepare structured claim and invoice data from fulfilled orders and supporting documents. Submission depends on the agreed clearinghouse and payer setup.

  • Claim header and service-line context
  • Order, delivery and document linkage
  • External submission setup confirmed at onboarding

06 / Payments & reconciliation

Give every payment its context.

Record payments, adjustments and write-offs at claim or service-line level and review outstanding balances.

  • Claim and line-level payment posting
  • Paid, partial and outstanding visibility
  • Adjustments and reconciliation context

07 / Denials & follow-up

Follow the denial back to the source.

Capture denial reasons and status codes, link them to the claim and supporting documents, and track the next action.

  • Structured denial reasons and status
  • Claim and service-line linkage
  • Resolution tracking with upstream context

What is SunDME?

SunDME is DME/HME revenue cycle management software that connects patient intake, documents, orders, delivery, billing and follow-up.

Who is SunDME designed for?

SunDME is designed primarily for small to mid-sized DME/HME providers and their billing, revenue cycle and operations teams.

Which workflows does SunDME cover?

SunDME connects intake, document management, orders, inventory and delivery, claim preparation, payments and denial follow-up.

Does SunDME connect with our existing systems?

Supported integrations depend on your systems and project scope. Confirm EHR, EMR, clearinghouse and payer connections with the SunDME team.
