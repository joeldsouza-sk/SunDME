A little more clarity

Good questions.
Straight answers.

Here’s what to know before exploring SunDME with your team.

Who is SunDME designed for?

Primarily small to mid-sized DME/HME providers, billing partners, and the billing and operations teams who manage intake, documentation, orders, deliveries and follow-up.

Does it submit claims to every payer?

Claim preparation is a focus area. Submission relies on external clearinghouses and payer connections. Confirm the clearinghouse and payer connections for your environment during the demo and onboarding discussion.

How does it approach PHI and security?

Review the access controls, audit logging, encryption, PHI safeguards and applicable agreements for your deployment with our team before onboarding patient data.

Does SunDME replace my billing team?

No. The platform is designed to support experienced teams with connected records, workflow guidance and structured tracking. It does not promise zero-touch billing or guaranteed reimbursements.

How long does onboarding take?

Onboarding is phased. Configuration, training, data readiness and integrations shape the timeline. The initial discussion helps define a realistic scope rather than a fixed rollout promise.
