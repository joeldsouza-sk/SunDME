Intake & documentation

A stronger DME workflow starts at intake

A practical way to keep patient, referral and order information connected before the next handoff.

Sample blog · Prepared for the SunDME website.

A DME order passes through several hands before a claim is prepared. When essential information arrives in separate inboxes, folders and notes, each handoff can create another search. A structured intake process gives the team a shared starting point.

Build the record around the patient and order

Keep patient demographics, payer details, referral information and order context together. Staff should be able to identify what the request is, who sent it and which supporting information belongs to it.

A document being present does not automatically mean the order is ready. Teams still need to review whether the information is complete, current and appropriate for the item and payer involved.

Use a checklist that makes missing items visible

  • Confirm patient and insurance information against the source supplied.
  • Link the referral or prescription to the correct patient and order.
  • Record the status of supporting clinical information and any authorization review.
  • Assign an owner and next action for each missing item.
  • Make the handoff status clear before moving the order forward.

Give exceptions an owner

“Pending documentation” is more useful when it includes the missing item, the person responsible and the next follow-up date. That context helps the next team member continue the work without repeating the original review.

Keep the handoff connected
