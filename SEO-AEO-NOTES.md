# SunDME search and answer-engine readiness — October 28, 2026

Implemented: factual answer-first product description; 5 concise homepage FAQs plus 17 full FAQs on the dedicated page, with matching FAQPage JSON-LD; Organization, WebSite, WebPage and SoftwareApplication entities; canonical URLs; social metadata; XML sitemap; robots.txt; HTML-rendered module text; a platform overview and FAQ landing page; descriptive image alternatives and local SVG sample screens; crawlable internal links.

Access remains owner-private. The login gate prevents public crawlers from reading the marketing pages. These code changes prepare the site; they do not establish indexing, rankings or AI citations. A robots Allow rule cannot override authentication. Obtain the owner's explicit instruction before changing the audience.

Sample blogs, fictional case studies and the historical comparison page use noindex,follow and are excluded from the sitemap. Replace sample material with reviewed, evidence-backed content before removing those exclusions. No fake reviews, ratings, prices, certifications, author credentials or customer outcomes are included in structured data. FAQ markup is semantic assistance; it is not a promise of Google FAQ rich results.
